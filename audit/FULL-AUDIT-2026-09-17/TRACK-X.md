## CX-X1 — Securities / investment-contract framing in the product and marketing

**SEVERITY** S1 (existential-flag) · likelihood certain
**TITLE** The product tells users they buy a "stake" in a "company" that pays a "dividend in real
stocks" — the four-part Howey shape (money / common enterprise / expectation of profit / efforts of
others) is described in the copy, not merely implied.

**WHERE** (quoted copy + file:line)
- `Census-one-pager.md:7` / `CENSUS_ONE_PAGER.md:5` — "**Census is a launchpad for taking companies
  public on-chain**" and "anchor it to real public stocks (NVDA, TSLA, COIN, GLD…)". "Taking public"
  is IPO language.
- `CENSUS_ONE_PAGER.md:7,32` / `Census-one-pager.md:23-24` — "a coin that **pays its holders a
  dividend in real stocks**" / "the coin **pays holders a dividend in real tokenised stocks**".
- `CENSUS_ONE_PAGER.md:78` — "Census lets you **own a stake in what comes next** … a token … **that
  can pay you real stock while you hold it**."
- `Census-one-pager.md:51` — "Most pads let you bet on a name. **Census lets you back a real
  company.**"
- `CENSUS_FEE_MODEL.md:4` — "**guarantees a creator fee on every trade**" ("guarantees" + promoter
  compensation).
- UI, `frontend/src/pages/Landing.tsx:46` — "**An early public market for ambitious internet
  companies.**"; `Landing.tsx:5,43` — the hero word "Companies" and "Companies become markets".
- UI, `frontend/src/pages/Home.tsx:45-46` — "**Every company, on a curve.**" / "Anchored to ETH or
  HOOD."
- UI, `frontend/src/pages/Coin.tsx:484` — "**Holders of $X earn a share of every trade, paid in**
  {NVDA, TSLA, …}. Connect to see yours."; `Coin.tsx:479` "**Your rewards**"; `Coin.tsx:513,517`
  "**Creator rewards** … Paid to the creator on every trade"; `Coin.tsx:608` "**Holders' share** …
  % of creator rewards".
- UI, `frontend/src/pages/Launch.tsx:431` field label "**Company name**"; `:438` "**One-line
  thesis**"; `:836` "Your **company**"; review group `:837` "Company"; `:774` "**Holders earn**".

**WHAT HAPPENS** A first-time visitor reads that they can buy into a "company", hold it, and receive a
"dividend" in real stocks paid pro-rata from trading activity generated by others. That is the
textbook description of a security (a profit-sharing instrument / investment contract). "V2/Maxima"
coins are the acute case: the `DividendVault` buys a basket of tokenised NVDA/TSLA/etc. and pays it to
holders — the copy explicitly ties returns to holding.

**PROOF** DERIVED (from the quoted strings, verbatim). The word "dividend" appears 15+ times in the
one-pagers (grep of `CENSUS_ONE_PAGER.md`, `Census-one-pager.md`). The UI itself is more careful — it
says "rewards"/"earn a share", not "dividend" — but "public market", "company", "own a stake" and
"Holders … earn a share of every trade, paid in [stocks]" remain, and marketing is discoverable.

**BLAST RADIUS** Existential. If a regulator (SEC / FCA) reads the one-pager, the "stock-dividend
basket" is the single most likely thing to be called an unregistered security or a swap on a security.
Every V2 coin ever launched, plus the platform and named team members (`CENSUS_ONE_PAGER.md:67` names
"the tech lead takes 10% of the 1% fee … plus equity"), are in scope. Tokenised real-world stocks add
a second regulator (the securities laws of wherever the underlying trades).

**WHY IT EXISTS** The differentiator was chosen to be "a coin that pays a real-stock dividend" — the
product's whole pitch (`CENSUS_ONE_PAGER.md:31` "the Census signature"). The risk is in the design,
not a copy slip.

**FIX** (direction, and what it costs) Get securities counsel before mainnet — this is a design
question, not a wording one. Interim de-risking: purge "dividend", "public market", "own a stake",
"back a real company", "taking companies public" from all copy; call the mechanism what the contract
does ("a share of trading fees, auto-swapped into a token basket the holder can claim"); add a
prominent "not a security, not investment advice, no expectation of profit" disclaimer; consider
dropping tokenised-equity baskets entirely (see the premise challenge in the main report). Trade-off:
this guts the marketing hook.

**TEST/CHECK** A copy lint in CI that fails the build if `dividend|public market|own a stake|back a
real company|taking .* public|guaranteed?` appears in `frontend/src` or the one-pagers; sign-off from
counsel recorded in `GATES.md` before the "dividend" story ships.

## CX-X2 — Trademark & impersonation: real companies are the menu; coin names are unfiltered

**SEVERITY** S1 (existential-flag) · likelihood certain
**TITLE** The app ships a menu of ~45 real listed companies (names, tickers, logos) as launch
anchors and reward baskets, lets anyone name a coin anything (no filter), and brands the whole thing
"Robinhood Chain" with Robinhood's logo — a ready-made impersonation kit with no takedown path.

**WHERE**
- `frontend/src/lib/stocks.ts:7-45` — the catalogue: `{ticker:"NVDA",name:"NVIDIA"}`,
  `{"AAPL","Apple"}`, `{"MSFT","Microsoft"}`, `{"TSLA","Tesla"}`, `{"COIN","Coinbase"}`,
  `{"GME","GameStop"}`, `{"DJT","Trump Media"}`, `{"SPCX","SpaceX"}`, `{"META","Meta"}`,
  `{"GOOGL","Alphabet"}`, `{"PLTR","Palantir"}`, … used as the Maxima "rewards basket".
- `frontend/src/lib/anchors.ts:45-53` — `HOOD` anchor `label:"Robinhood"`, logo hot-linked, comment
  "the stock-anchored model that is a live meta on Robinhood Chain: OPENAI, ANTHROPIC, NVDA, HOOD…".
- `frontend/src/components/StockLogo.tsx:38` — `src={\`https://financialmodelingprep.com/image-stock/${ticker}.png\`}` — the real company logo for any ticker.
- `frontend/src/config.ts:10` — chain name shown in the UI: `"Robinhood Chain"`; `:6` default RPC
  `https://rpc.mainnet.chain.robinhood.com`.
- `frontend/src/pages/Profile.tsx:84` — "withdraw from **the Robinhood app** or an exchange".
- `frontend/src/pages/Launch.tsx:432` — "Company name" input, `maxLength={42}`, no validation;
  `Launch.tsx:147` — the only check is `nameOk = draft.name.trim().length >= 2`; ticker is any
  alphanumeric string (`Launch.tsx:797` regex `[^a-zA-Z0-9]`→uppercase). Placeholder is a live
  trademark: "Iron Man Industries" (`:432`).

**WHAT HAPPENS** A user opens Launch, types name "NVIDIA", ticker auto-derives to "NVDA" (or types
"Robinhood"/"HOOD"), selects NVDA as the anchor, and the page renders NVIDIA's own logo next to it
(pulled from financialmodelingprep). Nothing stops the launch — there is no reserved-word list, no
blocklist, no impersonation check client- or server-side (grep of `frontend/api/*` found no name
validation). The coin trades on "Robinhood Chain", so the finished product looks like an official
Robinhood/NVIDIA token.

**PROOF** DERIVED + PROVEN. PROVEN: repo-wide grep for `moderat|takedown|blocklist|blacklist|ban|
dmca|infring|removeCoin|hideCoin` returned zero mechanism (only the checkbox copy of CX-X5). PROVEN:
`nameOk` is a length check only (`Launch.tsx:147`). DERIVED: with the menu + logo fetch + unfiltered
name, an impersonating coin renders fully-dressed.

**BLAST RADIUS** Existential. Trademark infringement / passing-off exposure to every marked company
in `stocks.ts` and to Robinhood specifically (the chain brand + logo + "withdraw from the Robinhood
app"). Note: Robinhood Chain is a real chain (`*.chain.robinhood.com`), so building on it may be
legitimate — but *using the Robinhood name/logo as product branding* and *letting coins impersonate
Robinhood/HOOD* is a separate, live risk. A single cease-and-desist can force a rebrand or takedown of
the whole menu.

**WHY IT EXISTS** "Anchored to real companies" is the pitch; the menu and logos make it concrete. No
moderation was built because the indexer/backend is unbuilt and moderation was never scoped.

**FIX** Confirm written permission to use the "Robinhood" name/logo and each stock logo, or replace
with neutral marks; add a reserved-name/ticker blocklist and an impersonation check at launch;
license a logo source or self-host only permitted marks; add the takedown path in CX-X5. Trade-off:
less "real company" gloss.

**TEST/CHECK** Attempt to launch a coin named "Robinhood"/"Apple" on testnet and confirm it is
blocked; verify each logo URL's licence is recorded; confirm the "Robinhood" brand usage is
authorised in writing.

## CX-X3 — No Terms of Service, privacy policy, risk disclosure, consent, geoblocking or sanctions screening

**SEVERITY** S1 (existential-flag) · likelihood certain
**TITLE** A financial-trading product that will hold real funds ships with zero legal surface: no ToS,
no privacy policy, no risk disclosure, no cookie/consent, no geoblock, no sanctions/OFAC screening.

**WHERE**
- `frontend/src/App.tsx:52-59` — the entire route table: `/home`, `/paper`, `/board`,
  `/coin/:address`, `/profile`, `/profile/:address`, `*→/home`. There is no `/terms`, `/privacy`,
  `/legal`, `/risk`.
- Repo-wide grep for `terms|privacy|disclaimer|not financial advice|consent|geoblock|sanction|KYC|AML|
  restricted jurisdiction|DYOR` over `frontend/src` returned only: the "subject to moderation"
  checkbox (`Launch.tsx:811`) and the phrase "in ETH terms" (`Launch.tsx:706`). Nothing legal.
- `frontend/index.html:1-17` — no cookie banner, no consent script, no meta legal content; `<title>`
  is just "Census".
- The only "consent" files on disk are inside the Privy SDK bundle
  (`frontend/dist/assets/AffirmativeConsentScreen-*.js`, `DelegatedActionsConsentScreen-*.js`) — third
  party, not Census's own.

**WHAT HAPPENS** A user in any jurisdiction (including sanctioned countries or US persons for whom a
tokenised-equity dividend may be an illegal security) can connect and trade with no gate, no
disclosure of risk, and no agreement they are bound by. There is nothing that says "you can lose
everything", "this is not advice", or "not available where prohibited".

**PROOF** PROVEN (grep + route table above; both returned the absence directly).

**BLAST RADIUS** Existential + per-user. No enforceable liability limitation, no forum-selection, no
disclaimer to point to when someone loses money; potential sanctions-regime and securities-jurisdiction
exposure from serving restricted users; GDPR/e-Privacy exposure from CX-X4.

**WHY** Legal surface was never built; the product is engineering-first and pre-mainnet.

**FIX** Before mainnet: ToS + privacy policy + prominent risk disclosure (linked from footer and the
connect/launch/trade flows), a cookie/consent story (currently nothing is stored beyond wallet state —
confirm), IP-based geoblock of prohibited jurisdictions, and an OFAC/sanctions address screen on
connect. Trade-off: friction; some are non-negotiable for a funds-handling product.

**TEST/CHECK** `GATES.md` gate: "legal surface present and linked from every value-transfer flow";
verify a footer link to /terms and /privacy renders on every page; verify a connect from a blocked
region is refused.

## CX-X4 — Privy collects email/phone/social PII with no notice, consent, or deletion path (GDPR)

**SEVERITY** S2 · likelihood certain
**TITLE** Email, phone and social-account PII is collected via Privy to mint embedded wallets, but the
UI gives no privacy notice, no lawful-basis consent, and no way to see or delete the data.

**WHERE**
- `frontend/src/privy.tsx:42-49` — `sessionMethod()` reads and displays
  `user.email.address`, `user.google.email`, `user.apple.email`, `user.twitter.username`,
  `user.phone.number` — i.e. those identifiers are collected and stored by Privy.
- `frontend/src/privy.tsx:25` — the *only* text shown at sign-up: "We create a wallet on
  {chain} for you. You can export its key any time." No mention of email storage, purpose, retention,
  processor, or rights.
- `frontend/src/privy.tsx:31-33,116-129` — embedded wallets are created for users-without-wallets;
  the private key custody sits with Privy.
- `frontend/src/privy.tsx:152-166` — `signOut` disconnects wallets and calls `logout()`, but there is
  **no account-deletion / data-erasure call**; `exportWallet` (`:165`) exports the key only.
- No privacy policy anywhere (see CX-X3).

**WHAT HAPPENS** A user "signs up with email" (`Coin.tsx` trade panel: "No wallet? Sign up with
email"). Privy stores the email (data lives on Privy's US infrastructure; Census is the controller,
Privy the processor). The user is never told this, never consents to it, and has no in-app route to
request access or erasure. Signing out clears the local session only.

**PROOF** DERIVED from the files above; PROVEN that no privacy policy or deletion call exists
(grep returned none).

**BLAST RADIUS** GDPR/UK-GDPR exposure for every EU/UK signer: Art 13/14 (no information provided at
collection), Art 6/7 (no lawful basis / no valid consent), Art 17 (no erasure path), plus a
controller–processor DPA obligation with Privy that is not referenced anywhere in the repo. Fines are
turnover-based; more realistically, a single complaint forces a scramble.

**WHY** Privy was integrated for UX (embedded wallets) without the compliance wrapper.

**FIX** Publish a privacy policy naming Privy as processor and the data collected/retained; add a
consent step referencing it at sign-up; expose a "delete my account/data" path (Privy provides an API
for this); record the DPA. Confirm where Privy stores EU data and whether SCCs are in place.

**TEST/CHECK** Sign up with a throwaway email on testnet (steps for Jamie — do not create the account
in-audit), then confirm a working deletion request end-to-end; verify the privacy policy is linked
before the email field.

## CX-X5 — "Subject to moderation" is a promise with no implementation

**SEVERITY** S3 · likelihood certain
**TITLE** The launch form makes users tick "subject to moderation", but there is no moderation,
report, flag, blocklist or takedown anywhere — so the impersonation of CX-X2 has no remedy and the
claim itself is misleading.

**WHERE**
- `frontend/src/pages/Launch.tsx:810-811` — `<input type="checkbox" …>` + "**I understand launch
  details and images are public and subject to moderation.**" (the `publicConsent` gate,
  `Launch.tsx:147-153,191`: "Accept the moderation terms to continue.").
- Repo-wide grep for `moderat|report|takedown|flag|abuse|blocklist|blacklist|ban|dmca|removeCoin|
  hideCoin` across `.ts/.tsx/.js/.sol` (excluding node_modules/dist/.vercel) found **no mechanism** —
  only this checkbox copy. No report button on `Coin.tsx`, no admin removal, no contract-level
  hide/ban.

**WHAT HAPPENS** A user is told content is moderated (implying someone reviews and can remove it).
Nobody can. There is no report button for a visitor who spots an impersonating or abusive coin, no
queue, no removal function, and — since coins are on-chain and the front end derives from logs — no
clear way to hide one even if a report arrived.

**PROOF** PROVEN (grep result above: the string is the only hit; no code implements it).

**BLAST RADIUS** Trust + legal. The claim "subject to moderation" that is not backed can itself be
called a misrepresentation; and when an impersonation/abuse complaint lands (CX-X2, or the P-track
XSS/phishing coins), there is no process and no named recipient — "who receives the letter?" is
answered by nobody.

**WHY** Backend/indexer unbuilt; the checkbox was added for optics ahead of the mechanism.

**FIX** Either build the mechanism (report button on every coin → a queue → a front-end
hide-list/denylist that the UI honours, plus a documented contact) or change the copy to what is true
("public and permanent; Census cannot edit or remove on-chain coins") and add a `report@`/abuse
contact. Add a DMCA/impersonation contact address.

**TEST/CHECK** Verify a visible "Report" affordance exists on `Coin.tsx` and routes somewhere a human
reads; verify a reported coin is actually suppressed in the UI.

## CX-X6 — Unlicensed third-party logos hot-linked for every stock/company

**SEVERITY** S3 · likelihood easy
**TITLE** Every stock and company logo (and the Robinhood logo) is fetched live from
`financialmodelingprep.com`, a commercial data vendor, with no licence and no self-hosting — copyright
+ trademark + an availability dependency.

**WHERE**
- `frontend/src/components/StockLogo.tsx:38` — `https://financialmodelingprep.com/image-stock/${ticker}.png`.
- `frontend/src/lib/anchors.ts:50` — HOOD logo `https://financialmodelingprep.com/image-stock/HOOD.png`.
- `frontend/src/components/StockLogo.tsx:36-38` (and `AssetLogo` `:13-34`) — used across the anchor
  picker, launch preview, coin page and rewards basket. Fallback on 404 is a coloured initials badge
  (`:18-24`), so a takedown degrades gracefully but reveals the dependency.

**WHAT HAPPENS** The app displays a third party's copyrighted logo images (and the underlying company
trademarks) by hot-linking. No licence to FinancialModelingPrep's image API is recorded in the repo;
hot-linking may also breach their ToS and can be rate-limited or blocked at any time.

**PROOF** PROVEN (the two URL constructions above, verbatim).

**BLAST RADIUS** Copyright/ToS exposure to the vendor; trademark exposure to each company (compounds
CX-X2); operational risk that logos vanish if the vendor blocks the referrer.

**WHY** Convenience — one URL pattern yields every logo.

**FIX** License a logo source or self-host only permitted marks; confirm FinancialModelingPrep's terms
allow this use; treat logos as trademarks (display factually, not as endorsement).

**TEST/CHECK** Record the licence in the repo; verify logos load from an owned/licensed origin.

## CX-X7 — (Note) No first-party analytics ship; Privy is the only undisclosed processor

**SEVERITY** S4 · note
This is the reassuring answer to question (5): grep of `frontend/src`, `frontend/index.html`,
`frontend/api` for `gtag|google-analytics|googletagmanager|segment|posthog|plausible|mixpanel|
amplitude|fathom|hotjar|sentry|datadog|dataLayer` returned **zero hits**; `package.json` has no
analytics dependency. Nothing tracks users first-party, and nothing is sent to an ad/analytics vendor.
The only third-party that receives user data is Privy (`@privy-io/react-auth`, `@privy-io/wagmi`),
which is undisclosed (CX-X4) and whose own SDK collects device/session data server-side. If analytics
are added later, they must be covered by the (missing) privacy policy and consent.
